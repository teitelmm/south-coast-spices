How we use 'em

Tri-tip and roasted potatoes is the dinner these two were made for. Get the potatoes in the oven first, and they'll be ready right about when the meat's ready to slice.

Coastal Char on tri-tip

Steaks, burgers and chicken work the same way.

  1. Pat the meat dry.
  2. Coat it on every side. Don't be shy.
  3. Let it sit while the grill heats up.
  4. Sear it over high heat, then move it to the cooler side to finish.
  5. Give it 10 minutes to rest, then slice against the grain.

Coastal Roast on potatoes

Fries and veggies work the same way.

  1. Crank the oven to 425°F.
  2. Cut the potatoes into bite-size chunks, about an inch.
  3. Toss with olive oil and a good shake of Coastal Roast.
  4. Spread them out on a sheet pan so they aren't crowded.
  5. Roast for 35 to 40 minutes, flipping once, until golden and crispy.

Our story

It started with a favorite spice

When I was 17, my favorite seasoning disappeared. The company that made it went out of business, and nothing else on the shelf tasted the same.

So I set out to recreate it myself. That's how South Coast Spices got started.

Today it's two seasonings, Coastal Char for meat and Coastal Roast for potatoes, and every batch is still mixed by hand right here in Orange County.
